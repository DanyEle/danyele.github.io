# Chinese Course Credits

`hsk-vocabulary.js` contains the inclusive HSK 2.0 vocabulary lists for levels 1-5, adapted from [jelleverheyen/hsk-vocabulary](https://github.com/jelleverheyen/hsk-vocabulary), licensed under the MIT License.

That dataset attributes its old HSK word lists to `clem109/hsk-vocabulary` and its English dictionary definitions to CC-CEDICT via MDBG. The course keeps its original hand-written learning examples, grammar exercises, readings, listening checks, and podcasts.

The published syllabus counts lexical entries, while the bundled list deduplicates identical headwords. The resulting unique-word counts are 150, 297, 595, 1,193, and 2,493 for HSK 1 through HSK 5.
